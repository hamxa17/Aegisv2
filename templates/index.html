From request to delivery, in four steps

Every request follows the same clear path, so families know what is happening and coordinators know what comes next.

Submit

A family files what is needed and how many people are affected.

Verify

A coordinator reviews the request before it joins the queue.

Prioritize

Aegis scores urgency automatically and allocates stock by priority.

Deliver

A volunteer is assigned and marks the delivery complete.
